KeyNest — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

Data Storage

KeyNest stores all passwords and settings locally on your device, protected and encrypted using your PIN. We do not collect your data, synchronize it to the cloud, or upload it to any server.

Internet Usage

The internet connection is used only for:

  • Retrieving IAP configuration from https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N204.json
  • Processing purchases through Google Play Billing

Payments

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not store any payment card information.
